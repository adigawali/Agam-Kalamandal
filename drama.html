नाटक (Drama Troupe)

Power-packed competitive stage drama resembling premier inter-collegiate festivals like Firodiya Karandak and Purushottam Karandak, bringing intense social and artistic narratives to life.

स्पर्धा नाटक (Competitive Drama)

Rigorous scriptwriting, experimental stage design, and intense acting training tailored for prestigious state-level inter-collegiate competitions and cultural championships.

One-Act Play (Ekankika)

Compact, high-impact storytelling exploring deep philosophical, satirical, and social themes within tight timeframes that test raw acting versatility.

Street Play (Pathnatak)

Loud, vocal, and impactful performance arts designed for open-air spaces, driving social awareness and capturing public attention with high energy and live chorus chants.

Our Cultural Stage Legacy

The drama troupe is the beating heart of Maharashtrian collegiate theatre culture in Pune:

Karandak Circuit

Active preparation and participation targeting Maharashtra's most esteemed inter-collegiate theatre festival platforms.

Voice & Diction

Specialized workshops focusing on dialogue delivery, Marathi dialect modulation, projection, and stage presence.

Backstage & Direction

Hands-on mentoring in lighting, set design, background score composition, costume styling, and stage management.

Troupe Guidelines & Rehearsal Code

  • Script memorization deadlines and script readings must be respected meticulously to ensure smooth transitions into blockings.
  • Late entries disrupt creative flow. Punctuality during late-evening practice sessions is strictly monitored.
  • Members are expected to contribute not just as actors, but support backstage construction, props handling, and crew duties.
  • Commitment to represent Agam Kalamandal proudly across state-level dramatic competitions.
  • Casting Policy: Final role allocations and main character listings are determined transparently by the director and senior mentors based on capability, screen tests, and dedication.
