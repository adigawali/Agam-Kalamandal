The Vocal Arts Wing

From foundational shrutis and pure ragas to soul-stirring Natyasangeet and contemporary stage melodies. Cultivating pitch accuracy, breath control, and voice culture is at the core of our musical discipline.

Classical Gayaki

Rigorous training in pure ragas, vilambit and drut khayal, taans, and classical vocal mastery.

Natyasangeet & Natak

Expressive rendering of classical-based Marathi theatrical songs that define Maharashtra's cultural heritage.

Semi-Classical

Soulful execution of Thumri, Dadra, Abhangvani, and devotional compositions laden with deep emotion.

Modern Playback

Contemporary singing styles, microphone technique, acoustic styling, and light western harmonies.

Folk & Lok Sangeet

Vibrant rural traditions including Lavani, Powada, Gondhal, and traditional regional folk anthems.

Voice Culture & Riyaaz

Dedicated daily modules focused on base pitch extension, throat openness, and vocal flexibility.

Sufi & Ghazal

Mystical poetry rendition focusing on deep modulation, spiritual expression, and poetic grace.

Devotional & Bhajan

Harmonious group chanting and spiritual melodies designed for grand campus gatherings and cultural festivals.

Choir & Harmony Ensemble

Multi-voice polyphonic arrangements blending soprano, alto, tenor, and bass for powerful group performances.

Vocal Rules and Discipline Code

  • Strict punctuality and regular attendance for all vocal riyaaz sessions and vocal workshops.
  • Absolute decorum, silence during mentoring, and collaborative harmony inside practice zones.
  • Respectful attitude toward gurus, vocal leads, and accompanying instrumentalists.
  • Total dedication to mastering assigned vocal compositions for upcoming inter-collegiate competitions.
  • Disciplinary Action Policy: Any student found creating disruptions, showing consistent unauthorized absence, or flouting institutional rules will face immediate disciplinary action, which may include suspension or permanent removal from the Agam Kalamandal vocal troupe.
