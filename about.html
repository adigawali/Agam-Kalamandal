Foundational Excellence

M.E.S. Abasaheb Garware College

Established in 1960 by the historic Maharashtra Education Society (founded in 1860), Abasaheb Garware College has grown into a premier institution located in the heart of Pune at Deccan Gymkhana. The college stands as a powerful testament to uncompromising educational values, blending state-of-the-art scientific infrastructure with deep-rooted cultural values.

Within this dynamic academic landscape, Agam Kalamandal serves as the vibrant cultural heartbeat. We provide students with rigorous professional mentorship, rehearsal spaces, and performance platforms across classical vocals, folk arts, theater, and instrumental music, consistently dominating inter-collegiate youth festivals.

Our Living Legacy

Distinguished Alumni Circle

Garware College and the M.E.S. family have sculpted legendary leaders, iconic actors, classical maestros, and statesmen who shaped the modern nation.

Late Vilasrao Deshmukh

Former Chief Minister of Maharashtra & Union Minister

A revered statesman whose visionary governance and lifelong commitment to public service remain an enduring inspiration.

Late Vikram Gokhale

Renowned National Award-Winning Actor

A towering titan of Indian theatre and cinema whose unmatched dramatic mastery deeply influenced generations of performing artists.

Pt. Sanjeev Abhyankar

Classical Vocalist & Padma Shri Awardee

A virtuoso of Hindustani classical music (Mewati Gharana) who frequently returns to mentor and inspire campus vocalists.

Shri Prakash Javadekar

Prominent National Leader & Former Union Minister

A distinguished alumnus who has dedicated decades to national policy, public administration, and institutional governance.
