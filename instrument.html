Joining Rules & Discipline Code

  • Mandatory attendance at all scheduled practice sessions, camps, and rehearsals.
  • High level of discipline, focus, and decorum required during institutional hours.
  • Respectful conduct towards mentors, committee members, and fellow artists is absolute.
  • Commitment to represent Agam Kalamandal proudly at inter-collegiate platforms.
  • Important Equipment Policy: All instruments remain institutional property. Any proven negligence, rough handling, or physical damage will attract a direct penalty covering complete repair or replacement costs borne by the responsible individual.
